## 2. Declaration

- All AI-generated outputs were reviewed, tested, and corrected before inclusion.
- No AI tool was used to fabricate research data, test results, or references.
- All references in the manuscript were independently verified before citation.
- Provider names, contact numbers, rates, ratings, and reviews in `js/data.js` are fictional demonstration data and do not describe real people or businesses.
- Accounts, requests, feedback, and saved providers are stored only in the browser, and no confidential or sensitive information was uploaded to any AI tool.
